# CANDIDATES NOT INCLUDED

This document lists dataset candidates that were evaluated for the UniHack offline knowledge corpus but ultimately excluded, along with the reasons for their exclusion.

## 1. UNSPSC commodity taxonomy
- **Source:** UNSPSC
- **Reason:** LICENSE_UNCLEAR
- **Details:** Public viewing exists, but redistribution/offline retention terms were not accepted as clearly open.

## 2. ECLASS standard product classification
- **Source:** ECLASS e.V.
- **Reason:** PROPRIETARY
- **Details:** Standard/taxonomy licensing is not openly reusable for this offline baseline.

## 3. GS1 Global Product Classification
- **Source:** GS1
- **Reason:** LICENSE_INCOMPATIBLE
- **Details:** Terms are not a simple permissive open-data license for permanent redistribution.

## 4. Manufacturer PDF catalogs and datasheets
- **Source:** Various manufacturers
- **Reason:** PROPRIETARY
- **Details:** Publicly visible documents usually retain copyright and did not provide explicit redistribution rights.

## 5. CWRU Bearing Data Center fault data
- **Source:** Case Western Reserve University
- **Reason:** LICENSE_UNCLEAR
- **Details:** Useful for failure/time-series research, but license was not accepted as clearly permitting offline redistribution.

## 6. MIMII Dataset
- **Source:** Research dataset
- **Reason:** LOW_RELEVANCE
- **Details:** Machine-sound/anomaly dataset; not product identification/specification knowledge and large relative to value.

## 7. Kaggle industrial product datasets
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Reason:** LICENSE_UNCLEAR
- **Details:** Candidate datasets require per-dataset login/terms verification; skipped rather than assuming rights.

## 8. Wikidata
- **Source:** Wikidata
- **Reason:** UNAVAILABLE
- **Error:** HTTP Error 429: Too Many Requests
- **Details:** Approved source candidate failed during bounded acquisition due to rate-limiting by the provider.

## 9. Wikipedia
- **Source:** Wikipedia
- **Reason:** UNAVAILABLE
- **Error:** HTTP Error 429: Too Many Requests
- **Details:** Approved source candidate failed during bounded acquisition due to rate-limiting by the provider.
